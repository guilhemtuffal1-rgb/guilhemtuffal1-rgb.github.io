## 3. What makes Periplo US Edition stand out

### A complete deep-dive into American geography
- **50 states** + **District of Columbia** + **US territories** (Puerto Rico, Guam, American Samoa, US Virgin Islands, Northern Mariana Islands)
- **Official state flags** in high resolution, verified for accuracy
- **True state capitals** (not the largest cities — a distinction many competing apps get wrong)
- **Population and area data** sourced from the most recent official federal figures
- A rare find on the App Store: most US geography apps offer 1–2 game modes; Periplo offers **13**

### A deep and varied game experience
13 game modes designed for different types of learners:

- **Flags** — Identify the flag matching the state
- **Capitals** — Find the capital of each state
- **Largest** — Rank states by area
- **Most Populated** — Rank states by population
- **Organisation Quiz** — Recognize flags of associated organizations (federal, regional)
- **Quick Quiz** — Fast sessions, perfect for short breaks
- **Speed Round** — Race against the clock for experts
- **Blind Rank** — Rank without seeing the values
- **Daily Challenge** — A unique challenge every day
- **Campaign** — Progressive campaign mode, level by level
- **Rank** — Global competitive ranking system
- **Learning** — Pressure-free learning mode with full fact sheets
- **Profile** — Detailed stats, progress tracking, and achievements

### A truly global reach
The content is American, but the app speaks to the world: **14 languages** from day one — English, French, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Chinese, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Italian, Turkish, and Indonesian — with RTL support for Arabic. A unique way for an international audience to explore the United States in their own language.

### Attention to detail
- High-resolution state flags rendered via `expo-image`
- Native internationalization (`expo-localization`)
- Daily challenge push notifications (`expo-notifications`)
- OTA updates via EAS Update
- Smooth animations, modern design, iOS-native conventions

### Accessibility and inclusion
- **Free and accessible to everyone**
- No paywall, no locked content
- Designed for children from age 4 **and** adult enthusiasts
- Non-competitive learning mode for beginners

## 4. Story and inspiration

Periplo US Edition was born from a simple observation: US geography — one of the most taught and referenced in the world — is poorly served by mobile apps. Existing apps typically offer a single flags or capitals quiz. The goal was to build **the most complete experience possible** for all 50 states: 13 game modes, verified data, multilingual support, and professional-grade execution.

Developed entirely solo, with an obsessive attention to content accuracy — official state flags, correct capitals, up-to-date population figures.
