Periplo Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

What Periplo Publisher is

Periplo Publisher is an internal tool of Periplo, a geography quiz game. It runs only on the computer of the Periplo developer and is only used with Periplo's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

Data it accesses on TikTok

With the account owner's authorization (TikTok Login Kit), the tool:

  • uploads Periplo's quiz videos to the account as drafts, which the owner then reviews and posts in the TikTok app (scope video.upload);
  • reads the account's display name and its follower, following, like and video counts (scopes user.info.basic, user.info.stats);
  • reads the account's public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts (scope video.list).

It does not access other users' data, messages, or anything beyond these scopes.

How the data is used and stored

The counts are shown on a dashboard on the developer's computer to see which quiz formats people like. The data and the access tokens stay on that computer. They are not sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and they are not used for advertising.

Retention and revocation

Daily totals are kept on the developer's computer while the tool is in use. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions); the tool then stops reading or uploading anything.
