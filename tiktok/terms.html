Periplo Publisher — Terms of Service

Last updated: October 8, 2026

The service

Periplo Publisher is an internal tool of Periplo, a geography quiz game. It sends Periplo's own quiz videos to Periplo's TikTok account as drafts and shows that account's statistics to the Periplo developer. It is not a public service and no one else can sign up for it.

Use

  • The tool is only used with TikTok accounts owned by Periplo, with their owner's authorization.
  • Every video is reviewed and posted by the account owner in the TikTok app; the tool never posts publicly by itself.
  • Use of TikTok through the tool is also subject to TikTok's Terms of Service.

Content

The videos are made by Periplo from its own game data and graphics. Periplo is responsible for them.
