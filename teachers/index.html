Periplo

Quiz your class on the 50 States

Build a quiz on flags, capitals or the map, give your students one code, and follow a class leaderboard for 7 days. Free, no student accounts.

  1. 1

    Build your quiz

    Install Periplo US Edition on your phone or tablet. Open the Game Maker and pick what to practice: flags, capitals, population, size… Save it.

  2. 2

    Create the class

    Tap the class icon on your quiz. You get a 6-character code, valid for 7 days. Write it on the board or send the link to your students.

  3. 3

    Students play, you follow

    Students open Periplo, go to the Game Maker and enter the code. Each one gets a random nickname like “Clever Otter”. You see the whole class leaderboard on your device all week.

Nothing personal is collected

No account, no email, no name to type. Nicknames are generated by the app. A class stores only the quiz settings and each device's best score and time, under an anonymous ID; it closes after 7 days and is deleted within 30 days.

No ads during class games, and no extra lives: every student plays the same game. Outside class, players under 13 only get child-safe, non-personalized ads.
